# Privacy Policy — Deepfall Drill

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Deepfall Drill does **not** collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.

- The game has **no internet permission**. It works fully offline.
- Game progress (ore, upgrades, best depth, settings, checkpoint) is stored **only
  on the device**, in Android's private app storage.
- There are **no ads, analytics, crash-reporting or third-party SDKs**.
- The **VIBRATE** permission is used only for gameplay feedback (haptics), and it
  can be disabled in Settings.
- Uninstalling the game deletes its local progress.

Because no data leaves the device, there is nothing to request, delete or
export.
